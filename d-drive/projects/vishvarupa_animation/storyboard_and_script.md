# VISHVARUPA — The Universe Revealed
## 42-Second Timed Storyboard & Hindi Dialogue Script

> **Theme**: Mahabharata (Kurukshetra battlefield reveal of Vishvarupa)  
> **Target Runtime**: 42 Seconds  
> **Format**: 16:9 Cinematic Video (MP4)  
> **Language**: Hindi (Devanagari + Romanized Hindi for Voiceover / AI TTS)

---

## 1. Character Consistency Sheet

| Character | Key Visual Features (Lock in every prompt) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Lord Krishna (कृष्ण)** | Youthful serene face, glowing light blue skin, elegant ancient Indian golden crown with one prominent peacock feather (मोर पंख), yellow-gold silk garments (पीतांबर), ornate gold jewelry, calm posture. |
| **Arjuna (अर्जुन)** | Heroic young adult warrior, composed yet awestruck, traditional bronze-gold warrior armor, dark tied hair, bow (गांडीव) and quiver, regal brown cape/garments. |
| **Chariot (रथ)** | Ornate ancient Indian war chariot with four majestic white horses, golden flags with Hanuman crest, parked on Kurukshetra dust at dawn. |
| **Vishvarupa (विश्वरूप)** | Colossal divine cosmic form. **Key Rule**: Krishna does NOT stand beside a separate Vishvarupa; Krishna *is* the center of this expanding cosmic form containing stars, galaxies, oceans, and all life forms. |

---

## 2. Timed Scene Breakdown & Hindi Script

### Scene 1: Battlefield (0s – 5s)
- **Visual**: Kurukshetra battlefield at dawn. Sunlight piercing dramatic clouds. Ornate golden chariot in foreground with 4 white horses. Krishna holding reins; Arjuna standing beside him looking at the distant armies.
- **Audio / Ambience**: Soft morning wind, distant battle horns, low mystical drone.
- **Dialogue**: *(None - Establishing shot)*

---

### Scene 2: Arjuna's Question (5s – 10s)
- **Visual**: Medium cinematic shot inside the chariot. Arjuna turns toward Krishna with deep reverent emotion and uncertainty. Krishna remains calm and composed.
- **Audio**: Soft emotional musical build begins.
- **Dialogue (Arjuna)**:
  - **Hindi (Devanagari)**: "माधव... मैं आपको अपना सखा जानता हूँ। पर आप स्वयं को हर सृष्टि का स्रोत बताते हैं... यदि यही सत्य है, तो मुझे अपना दिव्य स्वरूप देखने दें।"
  - **Roman Hindi**: "Madhava... main aapko apna sakha jaanta hoon. Par aap swayam ko har srishti ka srot batate hain... yadi yahi satya hai, toh mujhe apna divya swaroop dekhne den."
  - **English Translation**: "Madhava... I know you as my friend. But you speak of yourself as the source of all things. If that is the truth... then let me see who you truly are."

---

### Scene 3: Krishna's Answer (10s – 15s)
- **Visual**: Cinematic close-up of Krishna's face. The environment falls unnaturally still. Leaves and dust pause in mid-air. Soft divine golden and blue light begins to glow behind Krishna.
- **Audio**: Ambience cuts to near silence; wind lifts fabric gently.
- **Dialogue (Krishna)**:
  - **Hindi (Devanagari)**: "तुम सत्य देखना चाहते हो, पार्थ? तो फिर इन चर्मचक्षुओं से परे देखो... जो केवल मैं तुम्हें दिखा सकता हूँ।"
  - **Roman Hindi**: "Tum satya dekhna chahte ho, Partha? Toh phir in charmachakshuo se pare dekho... jo keval main tumhe dikha sakta hoon."
  - **English Translation**: "You wish to see the truth, Partha? Then look beyond what mortal eyes can see."

---

### Scene 4: Transformation Begins (15s – 21s)
- **Visual**: Dramatic camera push toward Krishna's eyes. His eyes illuminate with intense divine light. Swirling cosmic dust, stars, and tiny galaxies emerge within his silhouette. Battlefield dissolves into deep cosmic space.
- **Audio**: Rising pulse, swirling wind SFX, deep resonant cosmic frequency.
- **Dialogue (Krishna - Reassuring Echo)**:
  - **Hindi (Devanagari)**: "देखो... मेरे भीतर ही वो सब है जो था... जो है... और जो सदैव रहेगा।"
  - **Roman Hindi**: "Dekho... mere bheetar hi woh sab hai jo tha... jo hai... aur jo sadaiv rahega."
  - **English Translation**: "Behold... Within Me is all that was... all that is... and all that shall be."

---

### Scene 5: Vishvarupa Reveal (21s – 31s)
- **Visual**: Massive continuous camera pull-back. Krishna transforms into the colossal Vishvarupa—a vast, infinite cosmic form occupying the sky. Galaxies rotate through the body; planets, oceans, and infinite forms of life glow within the divine silhouette. Battlefield below becomes tiny.
- **Audio**: Thunderous yet majestic cosmic soundscape. Celestial choir + deep bass impact.
- **Dialogue (Arjuna - Awestruck whisper)**:
  - **Hindi (Devanagari)**: "मैं आकाश... पृथ्वी... और समूचे ब्रह्मांड को आपके भीतर देख रहा हूँ... सब कुछ आप ही में है!"
  - **Roman Hindi**: "Main aakash... prithvi... aur samooche brahmand ko aapke bheetar dekh raha hoon... sab kuch aap hi mein hai!"
  - **English Translation**: "I see the heavens... the earth... every living being... Everything... within You."

---

### Scene 6: Arjuna's Realization (31s – 36s)
- **Visual**: Close reaction shot of Arjuna looking upward in profound awe and spiritual realization. Cosmic light reflects off his golden armor and eyes. Tears of reverence in his eyes.
- **Audio**: Music drops back softly to make Arjuna's realization clear.
- **Dialogue**: *(Arjuna breathes deeply in quiet surrender)*

---

### Scene 7: Final Cosmic Wide (36s – 40s)
- **Visual**: Extreme wide shot of the monumental Vishvarupa towering over Kurukshetra. Galaxies swirl slowly in blue, violet, and gold light.
- **Audio**: Deep reassuring divine voice over spacious cosmic bed.
- **Dialogue (Krishna - Final Words)**:
  - **Hindi (Devanagari)**: "उठो, पार्थ। भय को त्यागो और अपने धर्म का पालन करो।"
  - **Roman Hindi**: "Utho, Partha. Bhay ko tyago aur apne dharm ka paalan karo."
  - **English Translation**: "Rise, Partha. Cast away fear and follow your path."

---

### Scene 8: Cut to Black & Title (40s – 42s)
- **Visual**: Clean fade to black. Title appears in glowing gold text.
- **Text on Screen**:
  > **विश्वरूप — The Universe Revealed**
- **Audio**: Reverb tail fades smoothly into quiet wind.

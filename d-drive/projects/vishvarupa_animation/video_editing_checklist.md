## 1. Video Assembly Sequence (Timeline Layout)

1. **Import Clips**: Bring in your top animated takes for Scene 1 through Scene 7 into a 1920x1080 (16:9) 30fps or 60fps timeline.
2. **Timeline Structure**:
   - `00:00 – 00:05`: Clip 1 (Battlefield establishing wide)
   - `00:05 – 00:10`: Clip 2 (Arjuna medium shot)
   - `00:10 – 00:15`: Clip 3 (Krishna close-up answer)
   - `00:15 – 00:21`: Clip 4 (Krishna eyes push & cosmic particles start)
   - `00:21 – 00:31`: Clip 5 (Vishvarupa grand reveal pull-back)
   - `00:31 – 00:36`: Clip 6 (Arjuna realization reaction)
   - `00:36 – 00:40`: Clip 7 (Extreme wide Vishvarupa final shot)
   - `00:40 – 00:42`: Black screen with Title Card text overlay
3. **Transitions**:
   - Use **Cut transitions** (or subtle 0.3s Dissolve) between shot changes.
   - Do NOT use flashy wipe/zoom 3D transitions—keep it cinematic.

---

## 2. Color Matching & Consistency Fixes

AI-generated clips often have slight exposure or color temperature shifts from shot to shot. Apply these color adjustments:

- **Global Lut / Mood**: Apply a rich cinematic film LUT (Warm Gold highlights, Deep Blue shadows).
- **Match Exposure**: Adjust brightness/contrast so Krishna's blue skin tone remains consistent across Scene 2, Scene 3, and Scene 4.
- **Vignette**: Add a subtle 15% dark vignette around edges to focus attention on characters and the divine transformation.

---

## 3. Subtitles & Title Card Setup

### **Title Card (At 00:40s – 00:42s)**
- **Background**: Solid black screen.
- **Font**: Regal Serif / Ancient Gold font (Cinematic serif like Trajan, Cinzel, or Noto Serif Devanagari).
- **Text**:
  ```text
  विश्वरूप — The Universe Revealed
  ```
- **Color**: Warm metallic gold (`#FFD700`) with subtle glow.

### **Subtitles (During Video)**
- **Font**: Clean Devanagari font (Inter, Noto Sans Devanagari, or Montserrat).
- **Position**: Lower 15% center.
- **Styling**: White text with thin black outline / drop shadow for 100% legibility over bright cosmic effects.

---

## 4. Final Quality & Submission Audit Checklist

Before exporting your final file, verify each checklist item:

- [ ] **Runtime Check**: Video duration is strictly between **30.0s and 45.0s** (Target: 42.0 seconds).
- [ ] **Format Check**: Exported as **MP4** (H.264 video codec, AAC audio codec, 1080p or 4K resolution).
- [ ] **File Naming**: Saved exactly as your Enrollment Number (e.g. `210160107000.mp4`).
- [ ] **Character Consistency**: Krishna and Arjuna maintain identical face, crown, armor, and skin tone in every scene.
- [ ] **Transformation Integrity**: Vishvarupa is clearly Krishna's revealed cosmic form, NOT a separate person standing next to Krishna.
- [ ] **Audio Clarity**: Dialogue is crisp, loud, and clearly audible over background music and sound effects.
- [ ] **No AI Text Artifacts**: Ensure no raw garbled text appears anywhere inside generated background images.
- [ ] **Respectful Depiction**: Reverent, awe-inspiring, accurate to the grandeur of Bhagavad Gita Chapter 11.

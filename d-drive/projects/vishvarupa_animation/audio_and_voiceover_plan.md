# AUDIO & VOICEOVER PRODUCTION PLAN
## VISHVARUPA — The Universe Revealed (Hindi Edition)

An exceptional audio track is 50% of the impact of an animated short film. This document outlines the voiceover generation guide, sound design timeline, and mixing rules for CapCut / Premiere / DaVinci.

## 1. Character Voice Profiles & Settings

### **Lord Krishna (भगवान श्री कृष्ण)**
- **Tone**: Calm, majestic, deep, serene, commanding yet gentle and reassuring.
- **Pacing**: Measured, unhurried, clean Hindi pronunciation (शुद्ध एवं स्पष्ट हिंदी उच्चारण).
- **ElevenLabs / AI Voice Settings**:
  - Voice Model: Deep Indian Male / Regal Voice
  - Stability: `65%` (smooth, controlled)
  - Clarity / Similarity: `80%`
  - Style Exaggeration: `15%` (restrained, non-melodramatic)

### **Arjuna (वीर अर्जुन)**
- **Tone**: Younger adult warrior, humble, emotionally genuine, deeply reverent, awestruck.
- **Pacing**: Slightly tentative in question, breathless in awe during reveal.
- **ElevenLabs / AI Voice Settings**:
  - Voice Model: Heroic Young Indian Male
  - Stability: `50%`
  - Clarity / Similarity: `75%`
  - Style Exaggeration: `25%` (expressive emotion)

## 4. Audio Mixing Rules for Video Editors

1. **Dialogue Clarity (Rule #1)**:
   - Voice tracks MUST always sit `--4dB to --6dB` above the background music.
   - Use **Auto-Ducking** in CapCut/Premiere (duck music volume by `--10dB` whenever voice tracks play).
2. **Frequency Separation**:
   - Apply a high-pass filter on voice tracks (cut frequencies below 80Hz) to remove muddy bass.
   - Low-pass filter music slightly during dialogue so speech frequencies (1kHz - 4kHz) cut through effortlessly.
3. **Reverb & Spatial Effects**:
   - Add a subtle **Cosmic Echo / Hall Reverb** (15% wet mix) to Krishna's voice during Scene 4 & Scene 7 ("देखो... मेरे भीतर ही..." and "उठो, पार्थ...").
   - Arjuna's voice should remain intimate and dry with minimal room reverb.
